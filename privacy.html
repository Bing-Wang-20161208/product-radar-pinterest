Product Radar Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 4, 2026.

Product Radar is a private, locally run product research tool used by its owner. It is not currently a service open to other users.

Information used

The current application stores product research and observations entered by its owner on the owner's local computer. If a Pinterest Trends API integration is approved and enabled, it will request aggregated trend keywords, region, growth metrics, weekly interest history, and retrieval timestamps through approved read-only access. It will not request private Pins or boards, post content, or access other people's Pinterest accounts.

Purpose and storage

Research data is used to display charts, document source evidence, and produce rule-based product research notes. Application data and any authorized API credentials are intended to remain on the owner's computer. Credentials are not published in the source repository or displayed in the public research page.

Sharing and retention

Product Radar does not sell Pinterest data or provide API access to third parties. The owner controls and may delete locally stored research data. The public information pages do not include an analytics tracker or account registration form. The page hosting provider may process technical access logs under its own privacy policy.

Changes and contact

If Product Radar later adds external users, cloud storage, or model-based processing of personal information, this policy will be updated before those features are enabled. For questions about this policy, contact the developer through their GitHub profile.
