Product Radar

Product Radar is a private research tool run locally by an individual developer. It helps its owner document product ideas, compare trend signals, view historical charts, and keep the source and date of each observation visible.

The current application records research entered by its owner. A proposed Pinterest integration would use approved, read-only Trends API access to study aggregated keyword trends and weekly interest history. API access has not yet been granted. Product Radar does not publish Pins, manage advertising campaigns, or offer Pinterest account access to other people.

The tool is for research support. Its rules and charts do not predict sales or guarantee commercial results.
